WHAT HAPPENS NOW:
=================

1. Browser visits /random or /api/random
2. Session ID generated (UUID) and stored in cookie
3. Server registers session with 100-photo pool (configurable)
4. Phase 1: Serve same-day photos sequentially (5 requests in test scenario)
5. Phase 2: Serve random photos (100 photos in test scenario)
6. Automatic transition: Back to same-day after quota
7. Repeat forever without duplicates within cycles
8. Session expires after 24 hours of inactivity
